Why Spaceflight Fires Defy Terrestrial Safety Physics

01 // SPHERICAL FLAMES (0.0g)

Isotropic Blue Spheres (L/W ≈ 1.0)

Zero buoyancy eliminates updraft (Earth 1.0g teardrop L/W ≈ 3.2, ~1850 K). 3D molecular diffusion forms dim-blue, soot-free spheres (~1380 K) that evade optical detectors.

02 // VENTILATION PARADOX (5–20 cm/s)

Low-Flow Forced Airflow Acceleration

Cabin HVAC fans (5–20 cm/s) supply fresh O₂ without blowoff cooling, peaking spread in 5–12 cm/s and dropping MOC below terrestrial 1.0g limits.

03 // PARTIAL GRAVITY (0.16g / 0.38g)

Lunar & Mars Hemispherical Flaring

Weak buoyancy draws O₂ inward while failing to lift plumes vertically, spreading flames laterally into wide domes (+240% surface contact area & bulkhead heat flux).

04 // COOL FLAMES (500–800 K)

Invisible NTC Oxidation & CO

Second-stage Negative Temperature Coefficient (NTC) burning occurs at 500–800 K with zero visible soot glow while releasing lethal Carbon Monoxide (CO) & aldehydes.

TWIN U-CURVE EXTINCTION: RADIATIVE QUENCHING (< 3 cm/s) VS CONVECTIVE BLOWOFF (> 35 cm/s)

Terrestrial fire intuition fails dangerously in space. In 0.0g, flames lose buoyancy and become dim-blue spheres. Mandatory cabin ventilation of 5 to 20 cm/s creates a flammability paradox, while 0.16g and 0.38g habitats suffer broad hemispherical flaring and invisible 500 to 800 K cool flames emit toxic carbon monoxide.

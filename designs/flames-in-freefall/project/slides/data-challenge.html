NASA PSI Flight Data & NASA-STD-6001B Calibration Pipeline

EXP 01 // ISS CIR

NASA SoFIE

sofie_pmma_burn_rates.csv

6.35 mm PMMA cylindrical rod burn rates across 0–50 cm/s forced flow inside ISS CIR; maps low-flow peaks & quenching bounds.

EXP 02 // CYGNUS

NASA Saffire I–VI

saffire_pressure_oxygen.csv

Large-scale (0.4 × 1.0 m) fabric & polymer fires on Cygnus under hypobaric (56.5–70 kPa) and 26–34% O₂ Artemis atmospheres.

EXP 03 // ISS MSG

BASS-II & FLEX-2

bass_extinction_limits.csv

Microgravity LOI/MOC limits across flight polymers (PMMA, Cotton-Fiberglass, Nomex, Silicone, Ultem, PTFE, Kapton) & cool-flame alkane droplets.

NASA-STD-6001B TEST 1 & TEST 2 CALIBRATION

Test 1: Burn length ≤ 15.0 cm, zero dripping. Test 2: Peak HRR ≤ 100.0 kW/m². Mapped to SFRS tiers: Class A (<20), Conditional (20–45), Waiver (45–70), Prohibited (≥70).

SI CALIBRATION PIPELINE

CSV ingestion (data_loader.rs) → SI normalization (kPa, cm/s, K) → Arden Buck MOC_eff & De Ris spread → 14-report NTRS vector corpus.

All physics thresholds and material tables are grounded directly in NASA Physical Sciences Informatics data: SoFIE PMMA rods on ISS, Saffire I through VI large-scale tests on Cygnus, and BASS-II plus FLEX extinction records, calibrated against NASA-STD-6001B Test 1 and Test 2.
